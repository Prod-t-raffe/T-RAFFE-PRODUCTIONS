The Journey

From the East Coast to Los Angeles. Troy Curtin (T RAFFE) founded The Sanctuary studio after honing his skills under Andy Pinkham at Revolution Sound Studio. With a background rooted in drums and collaboration, Troy brings a unique 432Hz-inspired sound to every session.

Now based in LA, T RAFFE collaborates with top-tier talent, including Avionne and DJW, under the mentorship of Grammy Board Member Greg Papania (SiNES).
